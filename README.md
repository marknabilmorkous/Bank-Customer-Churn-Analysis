# Bank Customer Churn and Retention Analysis

An interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze customer churn, identify key drivers of attrition, and help retail banks optimize customer retention strategies and minimize financial loss[cite: 2].

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
<img width="1279" height="813" alt="Dashboard" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4c42b307-c118-4409-a8d7-65a99d245574" />

The **Bank Customer Churn and Retention Analysis Dashboard** provides comprehensive insights into customer behavior, geographic distribution, and risk factors associated with customer churn across a portfolio of 10,000 customers[cite: 2].

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Customers:** 10K customers evaluated[cite: 2].
* **Churn Rate %:** 20% total customer churn rate[cite: 2].
* **Total Lost Balance:** $185.6M lost due to churned accounts[cite: 2].
* **Avg Estimated Salary:** $100.1K average estimated customer salary[cite: 2].
* **Zero Balance Rate %:** 36.2% of customers hold a zero balance[cite: 2].

### Core Visualizations & Breakdown
1. **Customer Distribution by Gender & Location:** Interactive map showing customer density across **France, Germany, and Spain**, along with gender demographics (55% Male, 45% Female)[cite: 2].
2. **Churn Rate by Activity:** Compares churn between active and non-active members (Non-active members exhibit a higher churn rate of 26.85% vs. 14.27% for active members)[cite: 2].
3. **Avg Age & Credit Score:** Shows that churned customers tend to be older (Avg Age: 44.8) compared to retained customers (Avg Age: 37.4), with slightly lower credit scores (645.4 vs. 651.9)[cite: 2].
4. **Churn Rate by Product Count:** Highlights a steep rise in churn as product adoption increases—jumping to 83% for customers with 3 products and 100% for those with 4 products[cite: 2].
5. **Lost Balance by Product Count:** Tracks financial loss by product tier, showing the highest balance loss ($130M) concentrated in 1-product customers[cite: 2].

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Takeaways

* **Product Over-Concentration Risk:** Customers with 3 or 4 products have extreme churn rates (83%–100%), indicating potential friction in multi-product onboarding or unexpected fee structures[cite: 2].
* **Engagement Matters:** Non-active members churn at nearly double the rate of active members (26.85% vs 14.27%), making activity-based re-engagement campaigns critical[cite: 2].
* **Demographic Vulnerability:** Older demographics (mid-40s) represent a higher churn risk and should be targeted with dedicated loyalty or wealth management retention offers[cite: 2].
